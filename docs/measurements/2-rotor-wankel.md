# 2-Rotor Wankel

> Four firings per cycle from two rotors: no valvetrain, no beat, just buzz.

2.6 L  4 cyl  10.0:1  ·  naturally aspirated  ·  idle 950 rpm  ·  redline 8800 rpm  ·  firing order 2

Recorded by `cargo run --release --example measure -- --markdown docs/measurements`. See [the index](README.md) for what the numbers mean and what they are for.
